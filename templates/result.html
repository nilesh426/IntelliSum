How the Summary Was Generated

  1. The document was converted into text.
  2. The text was divided into sentences.
  3. Stopwords and unnecessary text were removed during preprocessing.
  4. TF-IDF was used to determine important terms.
  5. Sentence relevance was calculated with cosine similarity.
  6. Similar / redundant sentences were reduced.
  7. The highest-ranked sentences were selected.
  8. Selected sentences were restored to their original order.
